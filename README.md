# Wildberries Ops

## Overview

Wildberries Ops is an AI-powered platform that helps Wildberries sellers manage customer interactions and create compelling product imagery more efficiently.

The MVP focuses on two core capabilities: **customer service**, bringing product reviews, customer questions, and buyer chats into streamlined, AI-assisted workflows; and **AI-generated product images**, helping sellers create and refine visuals for their product listings.

By integrating with the Wildberries API and keeping sellers in control of outgoing responses and published images, Wildberries Ops aims to reduce repetitive work, improve customer engagement, and simplify product content creation.

## MVP Features

### Customer Service

- **Unified inbox:** View and manage product reviews, customer questions, and buyer chats in one place.
- **AI-assisted replies:** Generate context-aware response drafts using customer messages and relevant product information. Sellers can review, edit, and approve replies before sending.
- **Conversation management:** Filter and prioritize unanswered interactions, track their status, and reply through supported Wildberries API workflows.

### AI-Generated Product Images

- **Image generation:** Create product visuals from uploaded reference images and text prompts.
- **Image editing and enhancement:** Improve backgrounds, lighting, composition, and presentation while preserving the product's key characteristics.
- **Variants and approval:** Generate multiple image options, preview the results, and select approved images for download or use in Wildberries product listings.

### Seller Integration

- **Wildberries account connection:** Connect a seller account securely with the necessary API permissions for customer service and product media workflows.

## Core Workflows

### Customer Service Workflow

1. **Connect:** The seller connects their Wildberries account and grants the required API permissions.
2. **Sync:** Wildberries Ops retrieves product reviews, customer questions, and buyer chats and displays them in a unified inbox.
3. **Prioritize:** The seller filters incoming interactions, identifies unanswered items, and opens a conversation or feedback item.
4. **Draft:** AI suggests a response based on the customer's message and available product or conversation context.
5. **Approve:** The seller reviews, edits if necessary, and approves the response.
6. **Send and track:** Wildberries Ops submits the approved reply through the relevant Wildberries API, checks the result, and updates the interaction's status.

### AI-Generated Product Images Workflow

1. **Select:** The seller chooses a Wildberries product or starts with a new product image project.
2. **Provide references:** The seller uploads product photos and describes the desired style, background, or scene.
3. **Generate:** AI creates one or more image variations using the references and instructions.
4. **Refine:** The seller previews the results and requests adjustments until an image meets their needs.
5. **Approve:** The seller checks that the image represents the actual product accurately and meets applicable marketplace requirements.
6. **Export or publish:** The seller downloads the approved image or uploads it to the product listing through supported Wildberries media APIs, then verifies the result.

**Seller control:** AI-generated replies are never sent, and generated images are never published, without explicit seller approval.
